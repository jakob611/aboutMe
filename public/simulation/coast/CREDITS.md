# Strunjan Coast materials

The terrain is modeled at human scale from visual and geological references.
No survey imagery or height data is redistributed. The [Slovenian maritime
spatial plan's environmental assessment](https://dokumenti-pis.mop.gov.si/javno/veljavni/02_rep_priprava/2192/2/gradivo/02_okoljsko_porocilo/221_OP_PPN_23122020_dodatek.pdf)
describes the area's flysch cliff and shelf.

The diffuse and tangent-space normal maps are WebP conversions of the 1K
photo-derived materials from Poly Haven. All four source materials are
[CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license):

- [River Small Rocks](https://polyhaven.com/a/river_small_rocks)
- [Cliff Side](https://polyhaven.com/a/cliff_side)
- [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04)
- [Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02)

The near-field stones and shrubs have [separate model credits](../props/CREDITS.md).
The sky, water, canopy, terrain and placement are authored for this scene.
