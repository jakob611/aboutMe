# Field Studies scene objects

These are Poly Haven models, provided under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).
They were simplified, compressed and rescaled at placement. They add detail to
the modeled coast and do not represent scans of individual objects in Strunjan.

- `rock.glb`: [Rock 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_09) by Jenelle van Heerden.
- `shrub.glb`: [Shrub 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03) by Rico Cilliers,
  first variant only.
